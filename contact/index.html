Questions people ask.

Short answers. If yours is not here, send it with the form.

What do you do?

Technical SEO audits, keyword research and on-page fixes. Each one ends with something you can open and check: a report, a sheet or a change log.

Do you need access to my site?

Not for the first audit. I crawl it from the outside, the way Google does. I only need access if you want me to make the fixes.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the site. After a baseline crawl I send a fixed quote, so you know the price before any work starts.

Can you promise rankings?

No, and nobody honest can. I promise a measured baseline, clear changes and a re-check against that baseline.

How fast will I hear back?

Within 48 hours, with what I can check and what I need from you.

What happens after the audit?

You get a ranked fix list in plain English. I can make the changes, you can hand the list to your developer, or you can do it yourself.
