What I do to your site.

Three jobs, each one ending in something you can open and check. Pick one or take all three in order.

GET /services/auditcrawled ✓ indexed ✓

Technical audit.

I crawl your site the way Google does and tell you what is holding it back.

what you get
  • A crawl of every page I can reach
  • A ranked list of problems: what's wrong, what it costs you, how to fix it
  • A plain-English summary you can forward to your developer
what i need
  • The site address
  • Google Search Console access if you have it (I'll walk you through it)
  • A note on which pages make you money
what it isn't
  • A guarantee of rankings
  • A pile of auto-generated warnings
  • Changes made without you seeing them first
GET /services/keywordscrawled ✓ indexed ✓

Keyword research.

I find the searches your customers type and match each one to a page.

what you get
  • A sheet of searches grouped by what the person wants
  • Which ones you can realistically win, and which you can't
  • Which page should answer each search
what i need
  • Your products or services in your own words
  • A few competitors you watch
  • The cities or countries you sell to
what it isn't
  • A list of the biggest keywords with no plan
  • A promise of traffic numbers
GET /services/on-pagecrawled ✓ indexed ✓

On-page fixes.

I change the words and structure on your pages, in batches, and measure each batch.

what you get
  • Rewritten titles and descriptions, cleaner headings, better internal links, structured data
  • Every change logged with before and after
  • A re-check against the baseline
what i need
  • Edit access to the pages, or a developer who applies what I hand over
  • Your go-ahead on the list before anything changes
what it isn't
  • A rewrite of your whole site
  • Bought links
  • Anything that breaks Google's rules
